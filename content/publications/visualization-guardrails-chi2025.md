## Abstract

The growing popularity of interactive time-series exploration platforms makes data visualization more accessible, but can also enable selective presentation of information and misleading charts. This paper introduces visualization guardrails: interventions in data explorers that expose cherry-picking. Across three crowdsourced experiments, the authors find that guardrails—especially those that superimpose contextual data—can encourage skepticism, and offer recommendations for designing more effective guardrails.
