## Abstract

Social-media users often share data visualizations and interpretations that can be inaccurate or misleading. This paper uses a mixed-methods analysis of public engagement with COVID-19 visualization posts on Twitter to examine whether audiences amplify or challenge misleading interpretations. Posts with misleading visualizations received more replies identifying nuanced fallacies and caveats, highlighting both opportunities and limits for crowdsourced responses to data-driven misinformation.
