## Abstract

Deceptive data representations can produce inaccurate interpretations while appearing to offer data-driven insight. This paper analyzes Twitter posts about COVID-19 that contain data visualizations and finds that violations of visualization design guidelines are not the dominant way people mislead with charts. The authors identify common reasoning errors and show how even accurately plotted visualizations can be framed to support misinformation online.
